How it works

Minimum term

Both plans run for a 12-month minimum. This lets us offer zero setup fee — build time is recovered over the term instead of charged upfront.

Payment

Billed monthly via EFT, due on the 1st. 7-day grace period. Site suspended (not deleted) after 14 days unpaid.

Early cancellation

Months 1–3: 50% of remaining months owed. Months 4–12: flat R1,500 exit fee. Fair for both sides.

Ownership

You own your content, branding and domain. Full website ownership and source files transfer to you once the 12-month term is complete.
